---

Dear ETH AI Center Fellowship Committee,

I am writing to inquire about the application process for the ETH AI Center Postdoctoral Fellowship and to express my strong interest in joining the program.

I am completing my Ph.D. at [Your University], specializing in edge computing and on-device AI inference. My research has been published at [Top Venue 1] and [Top Venue 2], addressing challenges in [e.g., efficient model deployment, edge-cloud collaboration, latency-constrained inference]. Alongside my core work in AI systems, I have a keen interest in interdisciplinary research at the boundary of AI and cognitive/brain sciences — specifically, how computational principles discovered in edge AI systems can inform our understanding of distributed biological computation, and vice versa.

The ETH AI Center Postdoctoral Fellowship's emphasis on interdisciplinary, collaborative AI research makes it an exceptional fit for my goals. I am particularly drawn to the opportunity to work across research groups — for instance, bridging the systems and neuroscience communities — in ways that individual lab settings rarely allow.

I would appreciate any guidance on the current application cycle, including key deadlines and the mentor-matching process. I am also happy to share my CV and research statement at your request.

Thank you for your time.
